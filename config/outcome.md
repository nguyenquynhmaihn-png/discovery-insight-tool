# Discovery Outcome & Scope

## Context
- Company: an ESG consultancy (example case)
- Product idea: proprietary SaaS ESG reporting software, sold as added value alongside existing consultancy services
- Stage: idea (pre-MVP). No product exists yet.
- Target customer (ICP): mid-sized companies in Europe, overlapping the consultancy's existing client base
- Primary users (hypothesis): sustainability / ESG staff inside those companies

## Learning goal
Understand how mid-sized companies prepare ESG reports today, where it hurts, and what they would need from software.

## Research questions (every insight is tagged to one or more)
- RQ1 — Pain points: What makes preparing an ESG report difficult?
- RQ2 — Current tools: What tools and processes do they use today (e.g. Excel, consultants)?
- RQ3 — Software needs: What would they need from software to support this?
- RQ4 — Desires: What outcome do they ultimately want from ESG reporting?

## Relevance rule
- In scope: an opportunity that answers at least one RQ for the ICP.
- Parking lot: anything else. Keep it; never delete. Scope may change.

## Participant types (evidence is weighted by type)
- target_customer — works on ESG/sustainability inside a company that fits the ICP. Counts toward sizing.
- adjacent_user — does ESG work but outside the ICP (e.g. NGO sustainability team). Reported separately.
- expert — advocate, key leader, consultant. Provides context and hypotheses, NOT direct customer evidence.

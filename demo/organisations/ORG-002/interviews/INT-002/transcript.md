# INT-002 — Finance Controller, Logistics Co. B (DEMO)

Interviewer: Can you start by telling me about your role?
P-002: I'm a finance controller at a logistics company in the Netherlands, around 350 staff. ESG reporting landed on my desk last year because nobody else wanted it.
Interviewer: Walk me through the last report you worked on.
P-002: A large customer sent us a supplier questionnaire asking for our Scope 1 and 2 emissions and some social metrics.
P-002: I didn't know which emission factors to use for our truck fleet, so I spent a weekend reading guidance documents.
P-002: In the end we paid a consultant to check my numbers, which cost more than I expected.
Interviewer: How do you collect the data?
P-002: Fuel cards give me litres per truck, that part is fine. HR data is in another system and I export it to Excel.
Interviewer: What was the hardest part?
P-002: Understanding what is actually required. Every customer questionnaire asks the same thing in a slightly different way.
P-002: I answered basically the same question four times last year for four different customers.
Interviewer: What would good look like for you?
P-002: I'd like a dashboard where I can see all our ESG numbers in one place.
Interviewer: What would you do with that dashboard?
P-002: Not sure, to be honest. Probably show management that we are on top of it.
P-002: Mostly I just want to stop redoing the same work every time a customer asks.

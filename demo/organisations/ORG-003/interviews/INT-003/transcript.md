# INT-003 — Programme Lead, Environmental NGO C (DEMO)

Interviewer: Tell me about your organisation's ESG reporting.
P-003: We're an NGO, so nobody forces us to report, but our funders increasingly ask for impact and emissions data.
Interviewer: Tell me about the last time a funder asked for that.
P-003: In March a foundation asked for our travel emissions. I pulled flight bookings from our travel agent's invoices and calculated it in Excel.
P-003: It took about a week because the invoices don't show distances, only destinations.
Interviewer: What tools do you use?
P-003: Excel and a free online carbon calculator. The calculator gives a number but I can't show how it got there.
Interviewer: Why does that matter?
P-003: Because the funder asked me to explain the methodology, and I couldn't.
P-003: Paid software isn't realistic for us. Our budget for this is basically zero.

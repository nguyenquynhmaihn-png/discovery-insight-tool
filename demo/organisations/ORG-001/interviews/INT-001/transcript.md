# INT-001 — Sustainability Manager, Manufacturer A (DEMO)

Interviewer: Could you tell me a bit about your role and the company?
P-001: I'm the sustainability manager at a packaging manufacturer, about 600 people, three plants in Germany and one in Poland.
P-001: I'm basically a team of one for ESG, with help from finance when the report is due.
Interviewer: Tell me about the last time you prepared your ESG report.
P-001: Last spring. It took me almost three months, and most of that was chasing data.
P-001: Energy data comes from each plant manager in a different spreadsheet format, so I rebuild everything by hand.
Interviewer: What happened when data was missing?
P-001: The Polish plant sent their electricity figures two days before the deadline, and I had to estimate the gap for one quarter.
P-001: Honestly, I was not comfortable signing off numbers I couldn't trace back.
Interviewer: What tools do you use today?
P-001: Excel. One master workbook with about forty tabs. If I'm off sick, nobody else can open it and understand it.
Interviewer: Have you looked at any software?
P-001: We saw a demo of one platform, but it was built for large corporates. Too expensive and too many modules we'd never use.
Interviewer: If you could change one thing, what would it be?
P-001: I want the plant managers to put their data in once, in the same format, without me chasing them by email.
Interviewer: Why is that the most important?
P-001: Because the chasing is where I lose weeks. The analysis part I actually enjoy.
P-001: And with CSRD coming, the auditors will ask where every number came from.

# INT-004 — Independent ESG Advisor D (DEMO)

Interviewer: From your experience advising mid-sized companies, where do they struggle with ESG reporting?
P-004: Most mid-sized companies I work with don't know which framework applies to them, CSRD, GRI or voluntary standards.
P-004: They start collecting data before they know what they need, and then they collect the wrong things.
Interviewer: Can you think of a recent example?
P-004: A food producer I advised last year spent six months gathering water data that wasn't material for them at all.
Interviewer: What tools do they typically use?
P-004: Nearly all of them use spreadsheets. The software on the market is priced for enterprises.
P-004: I think SMEs would pay for something simple if it told them what to report and why.
Interviewer: Is it hard for them to get the data from other teams?
P-004: Honestly, for most of my clients that part is fine. Their finance system already holds the energy and travel spend, so they just export it.
